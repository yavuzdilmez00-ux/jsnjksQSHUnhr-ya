What Is an Online Healthcare Degree?
An online healthcare degree focuses on the systems, services, and roles involved in maintaining and improving patient health. These programs prepare students for careers in clinical support, healthcare management, public health, and specialized medical fields.
Students may study:

Healthcare systems and policies
Patient care and clinical support
Medical terminology and procedures
Health data and technology
Public health and wellness

Healthcare programs vary widely depending on whether the focus is clinical, administrative, or technical.
