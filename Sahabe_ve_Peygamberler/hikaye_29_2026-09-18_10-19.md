Good post from the team behind Apache Sedona—especially the work from Jia Y., James Willis and Pranav Toggi introducing SpatialBench.

The discussion around spatial performance lately has focused on speed claims (like the recent Serverless SQL comparison from Databricks), but what stands out here is the focus on price-performance and query coverage, not just runtime.

Using SpatialBench, the results showed that while Serverless SQL performed well on one of the simpler queries, Sedona delivered up to ~6× better price-performance across several others and supported more of the benchmarked spatial workloads.

My takeaway: benchmarking spatial systems is tricky, especially when serverless layers hide the actual compute. Normalizing for cost and testing across different query complexities gives a much clearer picture.

Worth a read if you’re working with large-scale geospatial analytics.
