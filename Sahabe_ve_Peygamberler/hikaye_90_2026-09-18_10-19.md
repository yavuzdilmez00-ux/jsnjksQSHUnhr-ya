## İçerik
The original Highland Electric van, Georgetown, 1970s. “Workmanship at its best.”

John Murray’s father starts wiring Georgetown homes and businesses under the Highland Electric name, industrial, commercial, and residential from day one.

After two decades as Georgetown’s electrician, the shop becomes Georgetown Electric. Same phone habits, same standards, clearer name.

John, who apprenticed under his father and worked beside him until his retirement, takes ownership. Master Electrician, RSE, and still the person who quotes your job.

EV chargers, 200 amp services, heat pumps, and smart homes are rewriting what a home needs. The shop’s answer is the same as ever: size it right, wire it properly, inspect everything.

John specializes in residential and commercial electrical installations: service upgrades, full renovations, new construction, EV charger installations, water heaters, landscape lighting, and the heritage home retrofits this area keeps him busy with. He prides himself on high-quality, safe, honest, reliable, punctual, and friendly service, and answers his own email.

Every electrician on our jobs is licensed, insured, and trained, and every job follows the Ontario Electrical Safety Code. When a panel change or permit-required install wraps up, the ESA certificate lands in your records, not a drawer at the shop.

Georgetown Electric Inc. is accredited by BBB Serving Central Ontario and has pledged to uphold the BBB Accredited Business Standards: build trust, advertise honestly, tell the truth, be transparent, honour promises, be responsive, safeguard privacy, and embody integrity. Accreditation is renewed every year and is valid through July 31, 2027.
