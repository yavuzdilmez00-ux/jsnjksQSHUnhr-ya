In Windows 11, you can customize the taskbar color by changing the system theme settings. However, the options for color customization may be limited compared to previous versions of Windows. You can select a light theme, but the accent colors available might not always match your preferences exactly, as they can appear differently based on the overall theme and system settings.

Unfortunately, if the specific shade of pink you desire is not available, you may have to experiment with the accent color options to find the closest match. Additionally, Windows does not currently support a feature that allows for lightening or darkening the selected accent color based on the mode (light or dark).

If you are looking for more extensive customization options, consider using third-party applications that allow for deeper personalization of the Windows interface, including taskbar colors.
