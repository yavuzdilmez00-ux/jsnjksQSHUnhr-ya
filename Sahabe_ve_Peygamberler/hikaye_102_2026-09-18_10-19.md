## İçerik
This boutique hotel offers warm hospitality and quiet luxury in one of Chicago’s most beloved North Side pockets.

Situated at the intersection of Andersonville, Uptown, Lincoln Square, and Ravenswood, The Guesthouse Hotel celebrates the vibrant constellation of Chicago’s neighborhood culture. The property is ideal for groups, families, and those seeking longer stays, thanks to spacious suites outfitted with full kitchens, separate living areas, and private balconies with gas grills. The experience extends to the quaint, communal spaces, including a club room, library, and roof deck (with unhindered skyline views). The hotel is within walking distance of some of the area’s most popular dining and drinking spots, along with vibrant cafes and shops.

Husband-and-wife team Dave and Simona Krug opened the hotel in 2014 with a clear vision: to create a suite-based, luxury neighborhood hotel where guests "wander home" and locals hang out. Since then, the Krug portfolio has grown to include Colton House Hotel in Austin, Texas and Rock Creek Resort in Red Lodge, Montana.

The hotel features 25 suites, each one with a private balcony and designed to evoke an elevated, residential feel. Starting at 600 square feet, the one-bedroom suite features 10-foot ceilings, an open kitchen with stainless appliances and a granite breakfast bar, and a bedroom and bathroom with luxe furnishings. Two- and three-bedroom suites follow a similar layout, with additional sleeping quarters and bathrooms. The team has thoughtfully considered how to enhance the comfort and convenience of the guest stay—e.g., plenty of locally made snacks for purchase, and an onsite washer and dryer.

While there is no onsite restaurant, the hotel is within walking distance of some of the city’s best restaurants, bars, and cafes. The hotel lobby also features plenty of locally made snacks and beer for purchase.

The property helps to accommodate family travel needs with cribs, high chairs, sound machines, bath toys, and Pack-n-Plays.

Close to shops, restaurants, bars, and cafes, and not far from Wrigley Field and Lake Michigan.

Many members of the team have been with the hotel for years, and many live in the neighborhood, offering guests local expertise on how to explore the area.

Their accessible suite is their two bedroom, two bathroom suite, with a roll-in shower, accessible toilet, basin, grab bars, and roll-under sinks in the primary bathroom; a bathtub with grab bars, emergency water flow shut-off, accessible basin, and toilet in the secondary bathroom; a full kitchen with lowered cabinets, counter, and roll-under sink; hearing-accessible rooms with visual alarms; and a television with closed captioning. All rooms are accompanied by braille and tactile signage. Public spaces are fully accessible, and there is an elevator to all guest suites. Guide dogs and service dogs are welcome, and parking includes a space for accessible vehicles/vans, ramp access for wheelchairs, and an unobstructed path to the lobby entrance.

The hotel has a small but well-equipped gym, and offers curated wellness services through partners such as HigherDOSE and Heilbron Herbs. The team organizes private events and special programming throughout the year (including fun holiday activations). Self-park options are available for overnight guests for a nominal fee, and the hotel is dog-friendly (no additional fee).
