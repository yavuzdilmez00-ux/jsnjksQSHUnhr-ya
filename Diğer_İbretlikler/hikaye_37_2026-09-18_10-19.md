## İçerik
Call for little fashionistas and makeover enthusiasts. Get ready to join the beauty world with
  Yiv's best simulator game, Hair Salon. You will act as a Barber in a famous hair salon and craft
  perfect looks for all your customers. You will experience the whole hair-cutting procedure, from
  hair washing to hair dyeing, and hair ironing to styling. Whether you are a skilled hair designer
  or just a casual player interested in the makeup game, we'll help you become the ultimate hair
  styling master.

Hair Salon is not just a hair-cutting game; it offers more makeup options. Apart from
  hair-styling, you can also dress up your girl customer in beautiful suits, decorate her with
  accessories, and match your fashion design with hats or glasses. Don't forget to take a photo of
  your masterpiece design and share it with your friends and family. Always be brave to try
  something new and trendy in our hair salon game online.

Toca Boca Salon is a simulation game that is free to play. Besides, the game gathers nearly
