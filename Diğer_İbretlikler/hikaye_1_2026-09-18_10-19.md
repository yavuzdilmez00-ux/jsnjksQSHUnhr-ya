## İçerik
Dead circuits, tripping breakers, mystery switches. Diagnosed properly and fixed once.

Electrical faults rarely announce themselves politely. A breaker that will not stay on, an outlet that died over the weekend, lights that dim when the furnace starts: each one has a cause, and finding it is the job. We troubleshoot methodically, explain what we found in plain language, and fix the actual problem instead of resetting the symptom.

Some repairs are the first sign of a bigger issue. A panel that trips constantly may be at the end of its capacity, and repeated faults in older wiring sometimes point toward a panel change or a service upgrade rather than another patch. When that is the case we say so with numbers, not pressure. We also handle water heater repairs and electric heating, and keep commercial clients running with scheduled maintenance.
