# İbretlik Hikaye

**Kategori:** Diğer İbretlikler
**Kaynak:** https://us-chicago.us/hotel/guesthouse

## İçerik
The Guesthouse Hotel offers One-, Two-, and Three-Bedroom Suites. The large suites are fully self-contained apartments with full-size kitchens, relaxing living areas, and separate bedrooms. Each suite includes a private balcony with a gas grill, plus a washer and dryer. Suites feature individual climate control, wireless internet, and DirecTV cable.

The Guesthouse Hotel does not have an onsite restaurant; however, there are multiple dining options within walking distance. Tank Noodle, located in the Uptown neighborhood, is known for Vietnamese noodle soup. Provisions, also in Uptown, features charcuterie, cheese, snacks, and locally made gifts, craft spirits, beer, and wine.

Guests have access to a small gym equipped with cardio machines. A rooftop deck offers views. The lobby includes a library. The hotel provides board and card games upon request. They provide residential style garages with in/out privileges for parking for an additional fee. Pets are welcome, and the hotel provides complimentary beds, food and water bowls, and baggies upon request.

The Guesthouse Hotel is situated in Ravenswood, near Andersonville, Uptown, Wrigleyville, and Lincoln Square. The 'Argyle' Red Line station is about 5 blocks away. The Metra Ravenswood stop is about 15 minutes away at Lawrence and Ravenswood. The closest Divvy bike stations are at Clark and Leland (South of the Guesthouse) and Clark and Winnemac (North of the Guesthouse). Saint Ita's Roman Catholic Church is 1 km away, Lincoln Square is 2 km, and Magic, Inc is 0.5 km.

The hotel offers event spaces, including a Club Room (45 m²) and West Deck (65 m²). The Library (23 m²) accommodates up to 12 people. Group room blocks are available with as few as 5 suites. Free parking is provided for guests attending concerts at The Aragon Ballroom or The Riviera Theater. For families, children items, cribs, and high chairs are available. Check-in begins at 4:00 pm, and check-out is at 10:00 am.
