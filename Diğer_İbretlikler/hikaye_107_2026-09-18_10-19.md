## İçerik
Gas Station is a fun management game where you build the gas station of your dreams and rise as a true garage tycoon. Clear debris, rebuild and expand your station, and service all kinds of cars to keep customers happy. Unlock exciting upgrades, manage your business wisely, and take part in limited-time events like servicing high-speed racing cars. Can you turn a rundown stop into a bustling fuel empire?

Gas Station can be played on your computer and mobile devices like phones and tablets.
